What is CharCraft?

CharCraft is a high-performance, web-based image-to-text conversion suite. We designed it for developers, digital artists, terminal geeks, and content creators who want to convert photos, logos, or screenshots into stylized plain-text representations like ASCII art and Unicode Braille matrices.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike other online generators that upload your images to third-party servers, **CharCraft processes everything directly in your browser**.

We utilize the HTML Canvas API and standard browser JavaScript/TypeScript execution. This guarantees that:

  • Your photos **never** leave your local computer.
  • No server-side database records your images.
  • The tool operates completely offline once the static website assets are loaded.
  • No subscription, no email signups, and no data tracking.

Key Features

  • ASCII Art Generator: Custom mapping of image luminance to multi-density character ramps, preserving width and vertical proportions.
  • Braille Art Generator: Unicode dot cell grouping (2x4 pixels) that generates sharp, high-density line drawings and contours.
  • Real-time Controls: Instant sliders to adjust brightness, contrast, invert luminance, scale columns width, and adjust preview fonts.
  • Export Options: Instant clipboard copy, plain text (.txt) downloads, and colorized HTML (.html) downloads for styling in websites or articles.

Open Source & Static

CharCraft is built using Astro, TypeScript, and Vanilla CSS. It compiles to static files, making it lightweight, fast-loading, and perfect for static hosting solutions like GitHub Pages.
