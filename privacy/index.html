Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

1. Client-Side Image Processing Guarantee

At CharCraft, accessible from our GitHub Pages site, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. All image conversions, processing, and renders are executed **strictly client-side in your local web browser**. We do not upload, copy, store, or transmit your images to any external servers or backend databases. Once you close your browser tab, all image data is instantly cleared from your system memory.
